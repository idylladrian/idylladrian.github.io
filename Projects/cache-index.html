Who are we?

CACHE (Catskill Artists & Creatives for Housing Equity) is an emergent collective of artists, craftspeople, and creatives in the Hudson Valley and Catskills region who are committed to the fight for housing justice.

We are forming this collective because we see how the entanglement of arts and development leads to gentrification and displacement in the places where we live and work, and we refuse to participate in that pattern. We want to work together to reimagine what it means to be an artist in community.

We work closely with the Hudson-Catskill Housing Coalition (HCHC) and we are a member of the Housing Justice for All (HJ4A) coalition. Please click below to learn more about our relationships.
